Final report 
From our analysis, we identified the following key problems:

### ** Operational Issues**
- Manual processes lead to **slow service and inefficiency**  
- Staff must handle multiple tasks without automation  

### **Data Management Problems**
- Lack of a centralized system causes **data duplication and inconsistency**  
- Customer and booking records are difficult to update and retrieve  

### ** Booking Errors**
- High risk of **double booking rooms**  
- Room availability is not updated in real time  

### ** Limited Visibility**
- No clear overview of:
  - Available rooms  
  - Current guests  
  - Booking history  

### ** Billing Inaccuracy**
- Manual calculations can result in **incorrect charges**  
- Difficult to track payment history  

### ** Poor Decision Support**
- Lack of organized data makes it hard to:
  - Generate reports  
  - Analyze performance  
  - Make informed decisions
